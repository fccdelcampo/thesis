==================================================
 LLM ANSWER:
==================================================
D. mid-ocean ridge 

This is because a mid-ocean ridge forms as a result of tectonic plates diverging, creating new oceanic crust by volcanic activity and the upwelling of mantle material. This process occurs at the boundary between two moving tectonic plates that are pulling apart from each other.
