A trench does not form as a result of tectonic plates diverging; it forms when two continental plates collide and push up the Earth's crust, creating mountains or volcanoes on either side. The correct answer related to plate divergence would be B (rift valley), C (ocean basin), or D (mid-ocean ridge).
