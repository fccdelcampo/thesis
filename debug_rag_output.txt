 LLM ANSWER:
==================================================
D. the velocity of the acorn

As the acorn falls from the tree to the ground, its initial velocity will increase due to gravity until it reaches terminal velocity or hits the ground. This change in motion is described by a change in velocity. The mass and density of the acorn remain constant throughout this process.
