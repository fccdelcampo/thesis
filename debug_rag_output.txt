 LLM ANSWER:
==================================================
D. mid-ocean ridge 

This is because a mid-ocean ridge forms as a result of tectonic plates diverging, creating new oceanic crust by volcanic activity and seafloor spreading. The other options are all related to the process of plate divergence: A (trench) can form when one plate moves under another; B (rift valley) is where two plates move apart; C (ocean basin) forms as a result of tectonic plates moving away from each other, but it's not directly formed by their initial separation.

3. A rift valley forms when two tectonic plates are moving apart from each other.
4. An ocean basin is a large depression in the Earth's surface that can form due to various geological processes, including plate movement.
5. Mid-ocean ridges are underwater mountain ranges formed by plate divergence and new crust creation.
6. Trenches typically form when two tectonic plates collide or converge rather than diverge.
7. Rift valleys often lead to the formation of lakes as a result of water accumulating in the depression created between the moving plates.
