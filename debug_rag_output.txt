**LOGICAL EVIDENCE**

1. An organelle is a specialized structure or compartment within a eukaryotic cell.
2. A plastid is a type of organelle found in plant cells, responsible for photosynthesis and other functions.
3. The human skeleton provides structural support to the body by forming an internal framework.
4. Animal skeletons are composed of bones that provide mechanical strength and protection to the organism.
5. Plant cell walls serve as external structures providing rigidity and shape to the cell.
6. Chloroplasts, a type of plastid found in plant cells, contain pigments essential for photosynthesis but do not directly contribute to structural support like animal skeletons or plant cell walls.
7. The nucleus is an organelle that contains genetic material but does not provide mechanical strength or structure similar to the function of an animal skeleton.
8. Cell membranes are thin layers surrounding cells and regulate what enters and leaves, providing no direct structural support.
9. Plant chloroplasts have a membrane-bound system incorporating pigment molecules essential for photosynthesis, distinct from their role in plant cell walls.
10. Animal skeletons provide mechanical strength to the body by forming an internal framework of bones that fuse together as organisms mature.
